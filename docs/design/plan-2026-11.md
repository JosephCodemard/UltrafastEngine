## Findings from the code read that change the roadmap's wording

1. **Non-collinear OPA already exists with a stated angle** (`PhaseMatching.cs:86`, `Chi2Tests.cs:190` magic angle).
   What is missing is the *angle from the arms*: arm tilt becomes `ExternalRad` only for `sfg`
   (`CrystalElement.cs:781`); crossing overlap, smearing and output tilts are gated on `Process == "sfg"`
   (`:290, :345-370`); the idler's lab tilt is never recorded.
2. **Pump-probe pitfalls**: packets merge only with the same `SourceId` and if they fit one window
   (`MultiPort.cs:77-85`). A pump from a separate virtual input never interacts with the probe; a delay larger than
   ~half the window splits the packets and they propagate without cross-phase. The example must guard this.
3. **The thin-film transfer matrix** is `ThinFilm` in `Physics/Interfaces.cs:48-111`; coating kinds are a closed
   switch in `CoatingFactory.Create` (`:153-283`) with no `Register` hook.
4. **Crystal overrides already exist**: `base`, user index models and a `"general"` d tensor
   (`Crystal.cs:202-236, :375`); QPM has `periodExpr` and a domain-resolved model (`CrystalElement.cs:302-338`).
   Missing: duty cycle and explicit domain walls.
5. **Multimode tier 0 exists**: GRIN `indexProfile` on radial / cartesian grids (example 16).
6. **Self-steepening is one spectral factor** `(1 + Ω/ω0)` on the nonlinear term (`Nonlinear.cs:437-445`, fast path
   `:223-242`, GPU kernels): that is where a frequency-dependent effective area goes.
7. **The lab's "no-plate spectrum 1.5x too broad"**: 1.5 is exactly the linear / circular SPM ratio for electronic
   Kerr; the air tensor weighting is tested (`GasTests.cs:51`). First suspect: a polarisation mismatch between the
   recording and the model (owner question 11), then air n2 against Wahlstrand et al., PRA 85, 043820 (2012).

## A. T1.2 -- branches: the two open items

### A1. Pump-probe example (S-M)
Branched: unequal `beamSplitter` (R = 0.98), pump arm QWP +45°, probe arm QWP -45° plus `delay`, recombine, Kerr
plate, circular analyser, `spectrometer`; a delay study of the probe centroid. Counter-rotating circular light makes
isotropic χ(3) phase-only (no coherent artefact), so the result is exact:
δω(τ) = -(4/3) k0 n2 L I0 · 2τ Tp / (Tp² + Tr²)^{3/2} · exp(-τ²/(Tp² + Tr²)) (dispersionless, no Raman, weak probe);
extremes at τ = ±√((Tp²+Tr²)/2). Test at five delays, 1 %. Example asserts `packetsAtEnd == 1`. Stage 2 (optional):
two-colour pump with walk-off (Agrawal §7.4). Files: `docs/examples/41-pump-probe/`, one test.

### A2. Non-collinear OPA / DFG with the angle from the arms (M)
Generalise the crossing path from `sfg` to `opa`/`dfg` in `CrystalElement.cs` / `PhaseMatching.cs`; record signal,
idler and pump output tilts on the paths. References: transverse momentum k_p sin α = k_i sin β (1e-6); plane-wave
parametric gain G = 1 + (Γ/g)² sinh²(gL) (Cerullo & De Silvestri, RSI 74, 1 (2003)), 2 %; the cut from arm tilts equals
the stated-angle cut (1e-6); Manley-Rowe photon balance (1e-4). Example 42: a NOPA (SHG pump arm with tilt, seed from a
virtual input).

## C. Fibres (one serial worktree: all three touch the steppers and `CompilerFiber.cs`)

**C1 frequency-dependent A_eff (M).** Table or expression for `effectiveAreaM2` / `modeFieldDiameterM`; Lægsgaard
pseudo-field (Opt. Express 15, 16110 (2007)): nonlinear factor (1 + Ω/ω0)·[A_eff(ω0)/A_eff(ω)]^{1/4}. Tests: constant
table equals today's run (1e-12); photon number conserved without Raman (1e-6); a linear A_eff slope shifts τ_shock as
in Agrawal §2.3 and the Gaussian shock distance (Anderson & Lisak, PRA 27, 1393 (1983)), 5 %.

**Multimode, tiers (owner chooses):**

| tier | what | reference | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | GRIN MMF with the existing `indexProfile`: example + tests | self-imaging period π a/√(2Δ); geometric parametric instability (Krupa et al., PRL 116, 183901 (2016)) | S |
| T1 | few-mode GMMNLSE (Poletti & Horak, JOSA B 25, 1645 (2008)), `multimodeFiber` element, per-mode betas, overlap tensor given or analytic for HG/LG | one mode equals `fiber` (1e-10); HG00/HG10 overlap ratio 1/2 exactly; linear coupling cos² exchange | L |
| T2 | built-in mode solver (step-index LP, finite differences) feeding T1 and C1 | LP01 b(V) (Gloge 1971) | XL |

**C2 tapers (M).** Betas as expressions in z (or a `taper` table). The linear operator is diagonal in ω, so the
interaction picture stays exact with exp(i[B(ω,z2) - B(ω,z1)]), B = ∫β dz. Tests: GDD = ∫β2 dz (1e-9); adiabatic
soliton in exponentially dispersion-decreasing fibre, T(z) = T0 e^{-z/L} (Chernikov & Mamyshev, JOSA B 8, 1633 (1991)), 3 %.

**C3 saturated gain (L, needs C2).** `gain.kind: "rateEquations"`: steady-state two-level inversion from average
signal and pump power (co-pumped initial value, counter-pumped boundary value), g(ω,z) as a z-dependent linear term,
iterated with the propagated spectrum; reuses `GainCrystal`. Yb:silica cross-sections quoted from Paschotta et al.,
IEEE JQE 33, 1049 (1997) (read from the paper, not recalled). Tests: closed-form two-level steady state (Saleh et al.,
IEEE PTL 2, 714 (1990)) to 1e-4; exact photon balance; small-signal limit equals `gainDbPerM`. ASE out of scope.

material from its structure (that needs DFT / ab-initio codes, research in itself).

### First: Escoto et al., CLEO 2024 SF2O.8 (the NER paper)
The abstract gives: FS Kerr plates in air near zero dispersion per round trip, max B ≈ 15 rad, QWP / MPC / QWP /
polariser, 1D+1 model with two circular components, FWHM in the cell ~1 ps, metric = I(0) / I(+2 ps), operating points
P1 θ1 = θ2 = 9.8° and P2 θ1 = θ2 = 40°, P2 with better contrast because the two circular spectra complement each other.
The cell is not stated; the laser is Seidel et al., LPR 16, 2100268 (2022) (preprint arXiv 2105.05882: 900 fs, ~350 mm
cell with ROC 200 mm mirrors, three 1 mm FS plates, 31 round trips, 115 µJ, ~80 % after the polariser, 55 fs Fourier
limit). Used as a **labelled hypothesis** until confirmed.
What the figures show, read from the owner's copy of the PDF (2026-11-04; IEEE-licensed: the PDF and its figures
never go into the repository, only these read-off values with their figure panel):
- Fig. 1(b) simulation at **φ_nl = 10 rad** (not 15) over θ1, θ2 ∈ [-50°, 50°]: efficiency ~0 in a tilted ellipse
  around the origin along the θ1 = θ2 diagonal; maxima (normalised 1) towards (θ2, θ1) ≈ (-30°, +35°) and (+30°, -35°);
  the map has inversion symmetry (θ1, θ2) → (-θ1, -θ2). Fig. 1(a) measurement looks the same.
- Fig. 1(c) contrast improvement at +2 ps (log10 scale): high only on the θ1 = θ2 diagonal, rising from ~0 at the
  origin to ~8-9 near ±45°.
- Fig. 2(a) efficiency along θ1 = θ2 (normalised): ~0.55 at 3°, maximum 1 at ~10° (P1), minimum ~0.47 at ~26°, second
  maximum ~0.62 at ~35°, ~0.4 at 40° (P2), 0 at ~44°.
- Fig. 2(f,g): P1 spectrum strongly modulated (~1015-1050 nm, several peaks); P2 smooth, single-lobed (~1020-1058 nm);
  P2 pulse much cleaner (pedestal ~10⁻⁴ at ±300 fs vs ~10⁻² for P1). Pulse after compression ~100 fs scale.
These give "location" and "ordering" checks with tolerances of a few degrees.

## Execution: phases and worktrees

One integrator (the main session) merges branches, runs `/regen`, updates the shared files (`CHANGELOG.md`,
`PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `docs/testing.md`, the `ROADMAP.md` / `docs/roadmap.md` pair, the validation matrix) and runs
`/verify` before each commit. Worktree agents do not touch those files. A failing replication or component check is
triaged as a bug first (`/bug-report`), fixed with a test, and only then is the replication adjusted.

| phase | parallel worktrees (disjoint files) |
|---|---|
| 0 | push, fix the first CI run; paper research pass (verify parameters and headline numbers in full text, one table per topic) |
